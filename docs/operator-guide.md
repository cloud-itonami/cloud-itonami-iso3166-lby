# Operator guide — LBY

Portal: no verified investment-promotion-agency portal for Libya (the
Libyan Investment Authority is Libya's sovereign wealth fund, not an
investment-promotion agency) -- this actor's market-entry mechanism is
company/business registration and foreign-company-office registration.
Entity registration: Ministry of Economy and Trade's Commercial Registry
(السجل التجاري, confirmed by World Bank Doing Business 2020's own
"Company registry at the Ministry of Economy" citation) plus, for
foreign companies, the Ministry's own representative-office/branch
registration procedure. Engagements also clear the Ministry of Economy
and Trade's own LYD 150,000 minimum maintained representative-office
bank-balance floor gate before a filing can proceed. Additional
touchpoints: Office of Tax Department (stamp duty), Chamber of Commerce,
Social Security Fund / Ministry of Labor, and the separate foreign-
companies operating license. Human-gated filing only.
