# Business model — LBY

Independent market-entry compliance for Libya.

- No verifiable currently-operating agency administering Law No. 9 of
  2010 on Investment Promotion could be independently confirmed this
  iteration (domain guesses for a "Privatisation and Investment Board"
  / "General Authority of Investment and Ownership of Public Companies"
  both failed to resolve; WebSearch budget exhausted -- see
  `src/marketentry/facts.cljc` for the full list of sources checked,
  including the Libyan Investment Authority, which is confirmed to be
  Libya's sovereign wealth fund, not an investment-promotion agency,
  disclosed honestly rather than glossed over).
- Libya is NOT an OHADA member state (independently confirmed via
  ohada.org) -- company/investment law is purely national: Law No. 9 of
  2010 on Investment Promotion (General People's Congress; own Art. 27
  excludes oil & gas projects from its scope). Business/company
  registration in practice runs through the Ministry of Economy and
  Trade's Commercial Registry, confirmed directly by World Bank Doing
  Business 2020's own "Starting a Business - Libya" procedure detail
  ("Agency: Company registry at the Ministry of Economy").
- Additional registration touchpoints (World Bank Doing Business 2020's
  own text): Office of Tax Department at the Ministry of Economy (stamp
  duty); Chamber of Commerce; Social Security Fund / Ministry of Labor
  (at company-lease notarization); Office of Operations License of the
  Ministry of Economy (business operating license).
- The Ministry of Economy and Trade's own live foreign-company-
  representative-office registration-requirements page documents a
  minimum maintained bank-account balance of LYD 150,000, funded via
  transfers from the parent company -- this vertical's flagship governor
  check independently validates an engagement's own declared, maintained
  representative-office bank balance against this floor before any
  filing/submit proposal can commit.

## Trust Controls

Any actual Ministry of Economy and Trade / Commercial Registry filing
submission requires Market-Entry Compliance Governor clearance and
always escalates to human sign-off. A false or fabricated regulatory-
requirement claim is a HARD hold that no human approval can override.
